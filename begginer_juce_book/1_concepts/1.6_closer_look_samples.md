### lost of information
When using sampled data, we will make the assumption
that the signal is **band limited**
It does not contain fequencies that are higher
than half the sampling rate.

We need to make sure that sampling rate is at least twice
as high as the highest frequency that occurs in the signal.

The maximum freq the signal can contain is the **Nyquist limit**
in 44100 Hz sampling rate the **Nyquist limit** is 22050Hz
Frequencies higher than 22050 Hz won't work.

Aan ADC has an electronic circuit that filters out
all the frequencies that are too high for the chosen sampling rate
