Human brain registers between 20 and 20000 times per second .
As programmer we need to describe the computer speakers how to move,
a stream of 44100 or more numbers per second.

A DAC digital-to-analog, turns the stream of numbers (samples)
into electrical signals that move the electromagnetic coil in the loudspeaker.
The resulting air movement is what we call sound.

DSP programming is about dealing with those numbers.
In JUCE are values between -1.0 and +1.0
**floating-point** numbers.

![[waveform.png]]

The **amplitude** is on vertical axis, describe position of loudspeaker's diaphragm
at given instance in time. 
**Time** in the horizontal axis measured in number of samples.
Signal oscillates up and down but is centered around the amplitude value of 0
0.0 is the resting position of the speaker cone.

![[audio_segment.png]]

Individual point is **sample**
It takes 44100 of these samples to ouput one second of sound.
The 800 samples of the graph is 18 milliseconds worth.

The DAW will pass this list of numbers through all the plug-ins on the track
plug-ins take turns modifying the numbers in interesting way.
